36. [x] Research Dolby Atmos re-encode support — researched via web search. Conclusion: not feasible to build. Genuine Dolby Atmos encoding (E-AC-3/AC-4 with object-based 3D positioning that actually engages Atmos on certified hardware) requires Dolby-licensed, certified encoder tooling — open-source/ffmpeg implementations are explicitly blocked by proprietary cryptographic components, and even tools that produce spec-valid Atmos streams don't reliably engage real Atmos decoding without certification. This isn't a "haven't gotten to it yet" gap, it's a licensing wall a self-hosted hobby project can't cross. What the app already has (AVAudioEnvironmentNode HRTF binaural "Spatial Audio") is the actual closest achievable equivalent on iOS for a third-party app — functionally the same category of thing Apple's own system-wide "Spatialize Stereo" does. Recommend not pursuing real Atmos re-encoding; if desired, the existing Spatial Audio feature could be enhanced instead (that's a separate, tractable task from #9's crash investigation).
